## About This Opportunity

Internet Archive is a non-profit digital library and top-200 website at archive.org, maintaining over 150 petabytes of digital information across self-owned and operated data centers. The Backend Software Engineer role sits on the Archiving & Data Services team, building and maintaining the core systems and applications that power IA's services for thousands of libraries, non-profits, cultural heritage organizations, and educational institutions worldwide. Notably, IA builds without cloud services—they own and operate their own infrastructure.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Mission Alignment** - Universal access to all knowledge is a cause worth building for
2. **Non-Profit Tech** - I've spent my career at a non-profit; I understand the constraints and the purpose
3. **Build Without Cloud** - Refreshing engineering philosophy that demands real infrastructure skills
4. **Scale** - 150+ petabytes and growing exponentially is a genuinely interesting technical challenge
5. **Open Source Culture** - IA's commitment to openness matches my values

I've consistently gravitated toward the systems side: building integrations, managing databases, writing the code that runs reliably in the background. My QA origins mean I think about failure modes, edge cases, and operational reliability as second nature.

At a place like Internet Archive, where the technology directly enables the mission, that combination matters.
