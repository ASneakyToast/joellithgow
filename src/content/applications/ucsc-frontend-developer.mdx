## About This Opportunity

This is a **UC Santa Cruz employee position** working on the **Smarter Balanced Assessment Consortium (SBAC)** platform—the online K-12 assessment system used by millions of students across 12 states and territories. SBAC is administratively housed at UCSC Silicon Valley Extension, meaning you're a university employee whose work serves this multi-state educational consortium.

**What you'd actually build:** Both internal tools (admin interfaces, content management) and external-facing software (assessment delivery, teacher dashboards, reporting tools). The role is 85% frontend development, 15% process improvement.

SBAC's mission drives the work:

> "A high-quality assessment system can provide information and tools for teachers and schools to improve instruction and help students succeed, regardless of disability, language, or subgroup."

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Educational Equity at Scale** - State assessments reach every student, including those with disabilities and language barriers. My accessibility work directly serves this mission.
2. **Accessibility Expertise Matters Here** - State assessments must be ADA compliant. This isn't a nice-to-have; it's legally required—and it's my strength.
3. **QA-Integrated Development** - The 85/15 dev/process split matches my background. I started in QA and still think in test plans and edge cases.
4. **Stable, Established Environment** - Mature codebase, documented processes, stakeholder-driven priorities. I understand how to work effectively in institutional settings.
5. **Remote Work** - Established remote practices from my current role.

**Honest about growth areas:** Angular and AWS are new to me, but I have a strong TypeScript foundation and GCP/Docker experience. I'm genuinely interested in learning within a structured environment.
