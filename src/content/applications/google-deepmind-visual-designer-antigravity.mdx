## About This Opportunity

Google DeepMind is building Antigravity—a next-generation product using AI agents to redefine how developers write software. This role creates the visual design language for that experience: typography, color, iconography, spatial systems, and motion that make complex AI interactions feel intuitive and beautiful.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **I'm the User** - I use AI coding tools daily; I understand what works and what doesn't
2. **Design + Engineering** - My career bridges both disciplines
3. **Systems Thinking** - Creating scalable design languages is my favorite kind of problem
4. **DeepMind** - Working at the frontier of AI research

## Unique Angle

Most visual designers applying for this role won't be daily users of AI coding tools. I am. I use Claude Code for:
- Building WebGL shaders and generative art
- Orchestrating complex development workflows
- Rapid prototyping and iteration

I understand the mental model of developers using AI assistants because I am one. I know where the friction points are, where visual clarity matters most, and what makes an AI interface feel trustworthy versus opaque.
