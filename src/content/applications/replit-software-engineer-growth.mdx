## About This Opportunity

Replit is building a Growth Engineering team to scale a product that went from ~2M to 250M users in under a year. The role sits at the intersection of engineering, product, and marketing — leveraging code, experimentation, and analytics to improve acquisition, activation, retention, and monetization. This is an early team with significant ownership over technical direction and product outcomes.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Early Team** — Joining a newly formed Growth team means outsized influence on technical direction
2. **Product Scale** — 2M to 250M users is extraordinary growth; optimizing the next phase is a compelling challenge
3. **Data-Driven Engineering** — I've done analytics-based redesigns and conversion work; this role formalizes that
4. **Cross-Functional by Design** — Growth engineering explicitly partners with product, design, and marketing — my natural working style
5. **Mission-Aligned Product** — I genuinely care about making software creation accessible

- **Formal experimentation frameworks** — I've done data-driven design decisions but haven't used tools like Amplitude or run statistically rigorous A/B tests at scale. I understand the principles and can learn the tooling.
- **SQL depth** — I've worked with PostgreSQL but haven't done deep cohort analysis or complex queries. This is a learnable skill.
- **Pure engineering years** — At ~2.5 years I'm slightly under the 3+ requirement. My total 7+ years including QA and design adds context that pure engineering years don't capture.

## Why Replit Fits Me

Growth engineering rewards people who think about users, not just code. My path — QA to design to engineering — means I naturally think about:

- Where users get confused (UX instinct)
- Where implementations break (QA instinct)
- How to build the fix efficiently (engineering skill)

That combination is hard to find in a single engineer and directly applicable to funnel optimization, onboarding flows, and activation improvements.
