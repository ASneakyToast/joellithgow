## About This Opportunity

Replit is the agentic software creation platform enabling anyone to build applications using natural language. The Design Engineer role bridges design and engineering — shaping the design system (Replit UI), architecting front-end solutions, and ensuring seamless user experiences across the platform. The role sits in Engineering and reports into the team building the technical foundation for accessible app creation.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Design Engineer is my exact title** — My career literally bridges design and engineering; this isn't an aspiration, it's what I do
2. **Design System Ownership** — I've built and maintained component libraries at CCA; leading Replit UI evolution is a natural next step
3. **AI-Native Platform** — As a daily AI dev tools user, I have genuine opinions about the UX of AI-assisted creation
4. **Creative Front-End** — The role asks for complex UI animations and interactions — my WebGL/shader work demonstrates this
5. **Mission Alignment** — "Enabling anyone with an idea to create apps" mirrors the accessibility work I've championed

## Growth Areas (Honest)

- **React depth** — Primary experience is Vue/Astro; React conceptual knowledge is strong but production experience is limited. The patterns transfer well and I learn frameworks quickly.
- **Scale** — My design system work has been for a single organization, not a platform serving millions. The principles are the same; the scale is new.
- **Mentoring engineers** — I've influenced design thinking across teams, but haven't formally mentored engineers on design principles at an org level.

## Why Replit Fits Me

Most candidates will be either designers who learned to code or engineers who appreciate design. I've done the full progression — QA, then design, then engineering — which means:

- I catch implementation bugs before they ship (QA instinct)
- I evaluate components as both a user and a builder
- I prototype in code, not just mockups
- I bring WebGL/shader craft that goes beyond standard UI engineering

Replit's focus on making software creation accessible to everyone resonates with my accessibility-first approach to design engineering.
