This role owns Replit's design system end-to-end — components, patterns, principles, and governance. It sits at the intersection of product design, design engineering, and platform thinking, with direct impact on internal velocity and enterprise adoption. The system is becoming a core part of Replit's enterprise value proposition, supporting customer-integrated design systems.

The role reports into Design and is remote (US).

1. **Design System Ownership** — Defining not just components but how the system evolves, how decisions get made, and how consistency and flexibility balance
2. **Design + Engineering Intersection** — The role explicitly requires partnering with design engineers on implementation — this is where I work naturally
3. **Enterprise Scale** — Supporting custom stacks, enterprise theming, and customer-specific design systems is a compelling platform challenge
4. **Governance & Contribution Models** — I care deeply about how systems scale beyond individuals through clear documentation and review processes
5. **Remote** — Practical advantage for me

**The requirement:** 10+ years of product design experience with deep design system ownership at scale.

**My reality:** ~4 years professional design experience, with design system work at a single organization.

**Why I'm applying anyway:**

1. **Hybrid skills are the differentiator** — Most designers at this level don't write production TypeScript. I do. That means I understand component APIs, implementation constraints, and adoption friction from both sides.
2. **The role description matches my thinking** — Governance, contribution models, extensibility, and balancing speed with craft are things I actively think about.
3. **Remote access** — Even as a stretch, this is worth pursuing because the role is remote and the design system focus is exactly where I want to grow.

- **Code-level partnership** — I can review PRs, discuss component APIs, and prototype in code alongside design engineers
- **Accessibility expertise** — WCAG compliance is built into my design system thinking, not bolted on
- **Documentation instinct** — I believe systems scale through clear documentation and rationale, not tribal knowledge
- **User perspective** — As a daily AI dev tools user, I understand developer-facing design system challenges

Replit's design system is evolving from internal tool to enterprise platform primitive — supporting customer-integrated design systems and theming.
