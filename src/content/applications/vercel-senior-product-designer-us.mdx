## About This Opportunity

The Senior Product Designer at Vercel designs experiences across the platform — the dashboard, deployment workflows, team management, v0 integration, and potentially developer-facing tools. Vercel's users are developers, which means the design challenges involve technical complexity, information density, and workflows that require deep domain understanding.

The "Senior" level suggests ownership of significant product areas, mentorship, and influence on design direction.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Designing for Developers** - Developers are a specific audience with specific needs. My background as both a designer and developer means I understand these users from the inside
2. **Data-Informed Design** - My homepage redesign was driven by analytics. Vercel likely has rich usage data to inform product design decisions, which matches my practice
3. **Design System Maturity** - Vercel's design system (Geist) is well-established. Contributing to and evolving a mature design system is a different challenge than building one from scratch
4. **Technical Product Complexity** - Designing deployment workflows, build configurations, and infrastructure management requires understanding technical concepts — my engineering background helps
5. **Remote Work** - Product design benefits from deep focus time. Remote flexibility supports the concentration this work requires

## Honest Assessment

**This is a stretch.** The role title says "Senior" and I've held a "Junior" and then mid-level title. The scope of product design at a company like Vercel is different from institutional UX design.

**Gaps to acknowledge:**
- **Seniority** — "Senior" at a well-funded tech company typically means 5-8+ years of product design experience. I have 7 years total but only ~2.5 years full-time, and much of it in higher education rather than product companies.
- **Product company context** — My design experience is institutional (higher ed), where stakeholders are internal. Product design for external developers at scale involves different research methods, faster iteration cycles, and different success metrics.
- **Mentorship scope** — Senior designers typically mentor juniors and influence design direction. I haven't formally mentored other designers.
- **Complex product flows** — Vercel's product involves deployment pipelines, build configurations, and team workflows. I haven't designed for this level of technical product complexity.

**What I bring instead:** A designer who codes is still rare in product design. I can prototype in code, evaluate technical feasibility, and communicate with engineers without translation. My analytics-driven approach and accessibility advocacy show design maturity beyond my title level.
