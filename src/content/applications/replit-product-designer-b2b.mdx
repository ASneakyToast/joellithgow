## About This Opportunity

Replit is seeking a Product Designer for B2B, working across AI features, collaboration tools, and software creation & deployment capabilities. The role offers ownership across all design levels — from strategy to UI details — with a focus on AI-native interfaces. The designer will contribute to Replit's Design System (Replit UI) and partner with Product and Engineering teams.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **AI-Native Interfaces** — Designing for AI-powered creation tools is genuinely new territory, and I use these tools daily
2. **Full Ownership** — Concept to delivery, strategy to UI details — this is how I prefer to work
3. **Design System Contribution** — I care about systematic design and have experience building component libraries
4. **Developer Tools Focus** — Advanced knowledge of developer tools is required; I use them daily as a developer
5. **Coding is a Plus** — The JD lists coding as "nice to have" — it's core to my practice
