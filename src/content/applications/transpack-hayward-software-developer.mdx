## Possible Fit Scenarios

### R&D Department (Stretch)
If the R&D team needs someone who can build software tools, testing infrastructure, or data pipelines to support hardware development — that's a legitimate match. Many hardware R&D teams need internal tooling: test harnesses, data collection systems, dashboards, process automation. The gap is domain knowledge in packaging/manufacturing, which would need to be learned on the job.

### IT Team (Moderate)
If the IT team needs a developer to build and maintain internal web applications, manage databases, or automate business processes — that's a more straightforward fit. Less exciting but more realistic.

### No Fit
If both teams need people with packaging/manufacturing domain expertise or specific hardware engineering skills (firmware, embedded systems, mechanical engineering) — this probably isn't the right opportunity.
