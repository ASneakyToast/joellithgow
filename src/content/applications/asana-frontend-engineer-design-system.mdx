My career progression is unusually well-suited for this:

1. **QA Specialist** → I think about edge cases, reliability, and how changes compound
2. **Interaction Designer** → I understand design intent and can translate it accurately

Most engineers come from one side of the design-engineering divide. I've worked on both sides, which is exactly what design systems work requires.
