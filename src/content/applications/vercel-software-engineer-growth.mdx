## Honest Assessment

**This is a stretch.** Growth engineering is a specialized discipline, and my experience with it is one project, not a sustained practice.

**Gaps to acknowledge:**
- **Growth engineering specialization** — I've done one analytics-driven redesign. Growth engineers run dozens of experiments continuously. I lack experience with experimentation frameworks, feature flags, and statistical analysis at scale.
- **React/Next.js** — Vercel's growth surfaces are likely built with Next.js. My production experience is Vue and Astro.
- **Experimentation infrastructure** — No experience with tools like LaunchDarkly, Optimizely, or custom experimentation frameworks.
- **Retention/activation metrics** — My analytics experience is primarily acquisition-focused (homepage). I haven't worked with activation funnels, cohort analysis, or retention modeling.
- **Scale** — My optimization was for thousands of visitors. Vercel's growth team optimizes for millions.

**What I bring instead:** The design background gives me a user psychology perspective that pure engineers often lack. I don't just look at where the funnel drops — I think about why users hesitate, what the signup form communicates, and how the onboarding experience builds (or erodes) trust. That qualitative lens complements quantitative growth methods.
