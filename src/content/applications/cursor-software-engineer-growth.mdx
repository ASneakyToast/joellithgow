The role seeks engineers with an entrepreneurial mindset driven by creating outsized business impact, interest in AI transformation, and the ability to blend engineering with design taste.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Onboarding is a Design Problem** - My UX background means I think about user journeys and first impressions naturally
2. **Business Impact Focus** - Growth engineering connects code directly to measurable outcomes
3. **Product I Use Daily** - I have genuine opinions about Cursor's onboarding and upgrade flows from personal experience
4. **AI Transformation** - Growth in an AI-native product means experimenting with novel conversion patterns
5. **Entrepreneurial Mindset** - Small team, outsized impact, fast iteration

## Why Apply Anyway

The posting seeks someone who blends "excellent engineering with a taste for models and design." My analytics-driven redesign work demonstrates the growth engineering mindset — identifying user friction through data, designing interventions, and measuring impact. The specific tools (experimentation frameworks, payment systems) are learnable; the instinct to connect engineering to business outcomes is harder to teach.
