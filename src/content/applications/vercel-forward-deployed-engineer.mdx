## About This Opportunity

Forward-Deployed Engineers at Vercel embed directly with enterprise customers to accelerate their platform adoption. FDEs help teams migrate to Next.js, optimize their Vercel deployments, integrate v0 into workflows, and solve complex technical challenges in the customer's specific context. The role requires strong technical skills combined with customer communication and the ability to work independently.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Customer Embedding** - Working directly with teams to solve their specific problems is more rewarding than building in isolation. My design background makes me naturally curious about user needs
2. **Platform Evangelism** - I genuinely use and appreciate Vercel's platform. Helping others succeed with it is authentic, not performative
3. **Variety** - Each customer engagement brings different technical challenges, codebases, and constraints. That variety keeps the work engaging
4. **Technical Breadth** - FDE work requires full-stack thinking — frontend, infrastructure, CI/CD, performance — which matches my generalist trajectory
5. **AI Integration** - Helping customers integrate v0 and AI-assisted workflows is an area where my daily Claude Code practice provides real credibility

## Honest Assessment

**The gap:** FDE roles typically ask for senior-level experience with extensive customer-facing work. I have ~2.5 years full-time professional experience, primarily internal-facing at one organization.

**What I bring instead:**
- UX design background creates natural customer empathy — I'm trained to listen, observe, and understand what people actually need versus what they say they need
- Real production shipping under constraints, not just personal projects
- AI-assisted development fluency that's increasingly relevant as customers adopt v0 and AI workflows
- Comfort with ambiguity from working across design, engineering, and strategy

**My angle:** Customer empathy is harder to teach than technical depth. My design training means I naturally approach customer problems with curiosity rather than assumptions, and I can communicate solutions without jargon.
