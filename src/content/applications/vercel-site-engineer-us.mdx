## About This Opportunity

The Site Engineer role at Vercel likely focuses on vercel.com itself — the marketing site, landing pages, product pages, and potentially the blog and documentation surfaces. Vercel's site is a showcase of modern web craft: clean animations, sharp typography, and fast performance. It's also a critical business asset — the front door for every developer evaluating the platform.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **The Site Is the Product Demo** - vercel.com doesn't just describe the platform, it demonstrates it. Building a site that proves Vercel's value through its own performance and craft is a compelling challenge
2. **Design Craft at Scale** - Vercel's site has some of the best interaction design on the web. Contributing to that level of polish is where I want to be
3. **Performance Matters** - A slow vercel.com would undermine the entire brand promise. Performance isn't a nice-to-have here — it's existential
4. **Modern Stack** - Building with Next.js on Vercel's own infrastructure, using the latest features firsthand
5. **Remote US** - The flexibility to work remotely while contributing to a high-visibility surface

## Honest Assessment

**Strong match:** Building marketing websites is literally what I do. The homepage redesign and After Intelligence projects are direct demonstrations of this skill — research, design, implementation, and optimization of high-visibility web surfaces.

**Gaps to acknowledge:**
- **Next.js/React production** — vercel.com is built with Next.js. My production experience is Astro and Vue. The component model transfers, but I'd need to learn Next.js-specific patterns (App Router, Server Components, ISR).
- **Traffic scale** — CCA's site gets thousands of visitors. vercel.com gets millions. I haven't optimized for that scale of traffic.
- **Team size** — I've worked on small teams (2-4 people). Vercel's site team may be larger with more established processes.

**What I bring instead:** The design-development combination means I'm faster and more autonomous than a pure engineer. I can identify design issues during implementation, propose solutions, and ship without waiting for a designer to update the Figma. That velocity is valuable on a marketing site where campaigns move fast.
