## About This Opportunity

Product engineers at Cursor develop software for the editor and core product team. The role blends research, design, and engineering to "invent a new, better way to build software." Example projects include designing UX for reviewing AI-generated pull requests, prototyping new product verticals like AI bug detection, and running A/B tests on millions of users.

They want engineers who have "built a great product" and blend "excellent engineering with a taste for models and design."

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Product Engineering, Not Just Engineering** - The emphasis on taste and design alongside engineering is how I naturally work
2. **AI-First Product** - Building AI features for developers who build with AI — I'm in this loop daily
3. **Prototyping Culture** - Rapidly prototyping new product verticals matches my build-fast instinct
4. **Scale** - A/B testing on millions of users is a step up from my current work
5. **Flat Organization** - Small team, direct impact, spirited debate

- **Pure engineering depth** — I'm stronger on the product/frontend side than deep systems engineering
- **Large-scale consumer products** — My production work is at institutional scale, not millions of users

## Why This Role Fits Me

The posting says "propensity for creative ideas" and "powerful tools without compromising ease-of-use." That's exactly the tension I navigate daily — building complex systems (WebGL shaders, AI workflows, CMS automation) while keeping the user experience simple. My design background isn't a detour from engineering; it's what makes me a product engineer rather than just an engineer.
