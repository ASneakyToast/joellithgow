## About This Opportunity

Replit is seeking a Brand Design Lead to shape how the company is experienced globally. The role involves evolving the identity system (typography, color, motion, iconography), leading design for product launches and campaigns, and creating scalable brand guidelines. It reports directly to the Executive Creative Director.

Replit's mission is "Autonomy for All" — making programming accessible, collaborative, and powered by AI.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Systems + Storytelling** — The JD describes "a designer who thrives at the intersection of systems and storytelling" — that's literally my practice
2. **Motion Design** — Motion proficiency is listed as a plus; my WebGL shader and animation work goes deeper than most brand designers
3. **Cultural Activations** — I co-founded an art gallery in SF; I understand how brands show up in cultural contexts
4. **AI Narrative** — Replit wants to shape the cultural narrative around AI and software creation; I have genuine perspective as a daily AI tools user
5. **Creative Director Report** — Direct access to creative leadership for vision alignment

## Honest Assessment

**The gap:** 6+ years of brand design experience at leading agencies or tech companies. I have ~4 years of design experience, primarily in higher education rather than agencies or tech.

**What I bring instead:**

1. **Rare creative range** — Most brand designers don't write WebGL shaders or build generative art systems. I do. This means I can envision brand expressions that other designers would need an engineer to build.
2. **System-level thinking** — My design systems and component library work demonstrates the systematic approach that scales brand foundations.
3. **Active creative practice** — Co-founding an art gallery isn't resume padding; it's evidence of genuine creative commitment.
4. **Code as creative tool** — I can prototype motion, interactive brand moments, and web experiences directly — no handoff required.

## Why Replit Fits Me

Replit's brand needs to be "as iconic, inventive, and human as the product itself." That requires a designer who can think systematically and execute creatively — which is what my work demonstrates:

- **Systematic**: Design tokens, component libraries, accessibility standards
- **Creative**: Generative art, WebGL shaders, physics-based animation
- **Human**: Art gallery, fine arts background, genuine creative practice

The ability to bring brand expressions directly into code — motion, web animations, interactive moments — is a differentiator that most brand designers can't offer.
