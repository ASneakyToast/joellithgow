## About This Opportunity

Cognition's AI Enablement Engineer role sits on the Customer Engineering team and focuses on helping enterprise teams adopt Devin effectively. The role involves leading interactive training programs, pair-programming with engineers at various skill levels, identifying high-ROI workflows, and developing scalable enablement materials and playbooks.

This combines deep technical expertise with a teacher's mindset and consulting intuition — helping customers move from "we have Devin" to "Devin is changing how our team works."

## Why I'm Excited

1. **I've Done This Work** — I built an AI editorial workflow at CCA and trained non-technical staff to use it. The gap between "tool exists" and "team adopts it" is exactly what this role addresses.
2. **Daily AI Tool User** — I can teach from authentic experience, not documentation. I know where AI coding tools shine and where they frustrate.
3. **Teaching Mindset** — My QA and design background gave me strong communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences.
4. **Pair Programming** — I collaborate daily using AI tools as a pair-programming partner. Extending that to human pairs helping teams adopt Devin is natural.
5. **Scalable Materials** — I've written documentation, built playbooks, and created training workflows — the scalable enablement side of this role.

## Unique Angle

Most enablement engineers come from either pure engineering or pure consulting. I come from the intersection of design, QA, and engineering — which means I think about enablement differently:

- **From QA:** I identify where workflows break down systematically, not anecdotally
- **From Design:** I understand how people actually learn and adopt new tools (not how they should in theory)
- **From Engineering:** I can pair-program authentically and build custom integrations on the spot

I'm also one of the rare candidates who has genuinely changed how a team works with AI tools. At CCA, I built an AI-powered editorial pipeline and brought non-technical staff through the adoption curve — from skepticism to daily use. That's the exact journey Cognition's enterprise customers need to take with Devin.

## What I'd Build

Based on the role description, I'm particularly drawn to:
- **Interactive training programs** — Workshops that get engineers writing with Devin in the session, not just watching demos
- **Workflow identification** — Using my cross-functional background to spot high-ROI Devin use cases beyond obvious ones
- **Enablement playbooks** — Scalable materials that codify what works across different team structures and codebases
- **Pair-programming sessions** — Meeting engineers where they are, adapting to different skill levels and comfort with AI tools
