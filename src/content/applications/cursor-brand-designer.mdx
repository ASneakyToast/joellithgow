## About This Opportunity

Cursor is looking for a Brand Designer in New York to define and evolve the company's visual identity as it scales. The role spans product, marketing, and community — developing brand identity with scalable visual systems, executing across digital, print, video, and events, and designing for audiences ranging from experienced developers to designers exploring code.

They want a portfolio demonstrating strong visual, typographic, and strategic thinking, experience with technical or developer-focused brands, and art direction expertise.

## Why I'm Excited

1. **Developer Brand** - Designing for a developer audience requires understanding the culture, and I'm part of it
2. **Cross-Format Execution** - Digital, print, video, events — I've worked across all of these
3. **Visual Identity at Scale** - Evolving a brand during rapid growth is a compelling design challenge
4. **Motion + 3D** - The nice-to-haves (motion, 3D, web design) are things I already do
5. **New York** - Location works

## Honest Assessment

**What fits:** Visual design fundamentals (BFA), typography, cross-format execution, motion design capability, and technical depth that means I design implementable systems. The nice-to-haves (motion, 3D, web design) are strengths.

**Gaps to acknowledge:**
- **Brand strategy at scale** — I've implemented and maintained brand systems, but haven't led a brand evolution for a high-growth tech company
- **Agency experience** — My experience is in-house at a single organization
- **Art direction of external partners** — Limited experience managing external creative vendors
- **Brand voice and messaging** — More visual design focused than copywriting/messaging

**Why apply anyway:** The combination of strong visual design, motion capability, and technical implementation depth is genuinely rare in brand designers. Most can't code; most coders can't design. For a developer-focused brand, having someone who understands the audience from the inside is valuable.
