The role is remote within the US and likely involves working across marketing, product, and developer experience surfaces.

Vercel has a strong design engineering culture. Their site itself is a showcase of craft — clean typography, smooth animations, and thoughtful interaction design.

3. **v0 Intersection** - The opportunity to work alongside the team building AI-powered design tools while being a design engineer myself is a compelling feedback loop

## Honest Assessment

**This is my bullseye role.** Design Engineer describes exactly what I do — move between Figma and code, care about both the design intention and the implementation quality.

**Gaps to acknowledge:**
- **React/Next.js production** — My production experience is Vue and Astro. React concepts are familiar and I've used Next.js in personal projects, but I haven't shipped a large React production app. This is a framework swap, not a paradigm shift.
- **Scale** — CCA's traffic is modest compared to vercel.com. I haven't optimized for millions of pageviews.
- **Seniority** — Depending on the level they're hiring for, my ~2.5 years of full-time experience (7 years including student roles) may be on the junior end.

**Why apply anyway:** The design-engineering intersection is genuinely rare. Most engineers don't have formal design training; most designers don't write production TypeScript. My WebGL work demonstrates I can handle complex rendering, and the React gap closes quickly for someone already fluent in component-based UI development.
