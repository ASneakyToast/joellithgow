<DisplayStatement>

<ChallengeSection insight="Interactive brand experiences can communicate product differentiators (like sound profiles) more effectively than traditional marketing copy or static product photography">
How do you communicate a headphone brand's sonic identity through digital experience? A friend's headphone startup needed a landing page that went beyond traditional product photography and marketing copy. The brand's core differentiator was customizable sound profiles ('theme settings'), but static images couldn't demonstrate this auditory experience. We needed a way to translate sonic identity into visual, interactive form.
</ChallengeSection>

<ApproachSection>
I built an interactive prototype that lets users experience the brand's sound profiles visually before ever putting on headphones. The experience features a 3D-rendered headphone model that users can rotate and explore, paired with a theme-based visual system that changes aesthetic and mood for different sound profiles (bass-heavy, balanced, treble-focused, vocal-forward). Each theme transforms the visual presentation, creating a multisensory brand experience entirely in the browser.
</ApproachSection>
