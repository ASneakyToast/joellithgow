## Case Study Framework for Anthropic

When writing case studies, ensure each one addresses:

### 1. Problem Identification
- What business/user problem did you identify?
- How did you discover it? (research, data, stakeholder conversations)
- What was the operational impact of the problem?

### 2. Technical Approach
- What technologies did you evaluate?
- Why did you choose your approach over alternatives?
- What tradeoffs did you consider?

### 3. Collaboration & Stakeholders
- Who did you work with? (emphasize cross-functional)
- How did you gather requirements from non-technical stakeholders?
- Did you present to or train anyone outside your team?

### 4. AI/LLM Integration (Critical for Anthropic)
- What AI capabilities did you leverage?
- How did you integrate AI into existing workflows?
- What was your approach to evaluation/testing AI outputs?

### 5. Impact & Outcomes
- Quantify where possible (time saved, efficiency gains, adoption rates)
- If project was discontinued (e.g., CCA closing), frame as "validated prototype; discontinued due to institutional closure"
- Include what you learned even if not fully deployed
